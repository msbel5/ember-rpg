- Every generated entry must justify its existence in gameplay terms.
- New entries must improve combinatorial variety for world simulation, dialog, economy, combat, or quest generation.

1. Read the source JSON, creator output, and relevant consumer code.
2. Check schema fidelity, duplicates, cross-reference validity, tone consistency, and gameplay plausibility.
3. Reject entries that are filler, redundant, or mechanically incoherent.
4. Produce a review report with:
   - pass/fail
   - exact invalid IDs
   - schema violations
   - duplicate/near-duplicate candidates
   - suspicious balance/content notes
